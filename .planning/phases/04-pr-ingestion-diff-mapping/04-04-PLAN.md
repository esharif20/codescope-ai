PROPOSED runtime semantics: synchronous POST remains open until terminal status. Caller provides its UUID before POST, allowing simultaneous GET polling on that ID while POST executes in FastAPI's request thread pool. One active request, no queue or detached task. Status is ephemeral within one process; no durable recovery/restart promise. The deadline is cooperative, checked between bounded units and before publication, not hard preemption of Tree-sitter/native execution. Hold exclusive admission until actual work and cleanup finish; never treat cancelling an await/future as stopping a thread.
